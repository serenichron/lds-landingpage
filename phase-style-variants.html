Phase highlight — four alternatives

Same Before / During / After content from Lesson 1, restyled four different ways. Each removes the coloured vertical borders that look noisy when stacked, and signals stage through a different mechanism.

Variant 1 — Stage chip on tinted card

Pill tag carries the stage. Card itself sits on a faint wash of the stage colour. Quiet, no rules.

During · Acquisition + Guidance

What is often assumed

Most institutions plan for content development. But effective online learning requires something much more substantial: a fully designed course architecture.

What is actually required

Learning outcomes mapped to activities, assessments aligned to outcomes, multimedia production, accessibility compliance, platform configuration, QA testing.

A single online module typically requires 3–5× the effort of equivalent face-to-face teaching.
