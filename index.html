Three barriers stopping you from launching online programmes

And why most institutions struggle despite having great ideas

Your team can't scale

Your digital learning team supports the entire university. They have the skills but not the capacity to develop full programmes on commercial timescales.

Competitors are moving faster

By the time internal approval processes finish and resources are assigned, your competitors have already launched and captured market share.

Recording lectures isn't enough

Your academics know their subjects but don't know how to restructure content for online learners. Simply uploading lectures doesn't work.

Watch: Our approach

Online learning isn't a technical challenge. It's a teaching challenge.

Most institutions buy recording equipment. Smart institutions invest in Academic Transformation.

Classroom methods don't translate directly online. Content structure, learner engagement, and assessment all need rethinking.

That's exactly what we do. We guide your academics through this transformation whilst building everything they need to launch.

The cost of waiting

Lost revenue

Every quarter delayed means competitors enrol the students who should have been yours. Market share doesn't wait.

Damaged reputation

A poorly executed first attempt—uploaded lectures with no engagement—damages your institution's reputation for years. Students remember bad online experiences.

Exhausted teams

Overloading internal teams leads to burnout and poor results. Developing fully realised, impactful, and effective online courses involves far more work than just uploading lectures.

From first call to students enrolled: Our four-stage process

Clear stages with specific deliverables at each step

STAGE 1 | Duration: 2-3 weeks

Consulting & strategy

We meet with your team to understand your institution's goals, target learners, and existing resources. Through structured workshops, we identify opportunities and map out exactly what needs to be built.

You receive a strategic roadmap showing all courses to be built, timeline with key milestones, and resource requirements. This becomes your blueprint for the entire project.

STAGE 2 | Duration: 4-6 weeks

Piloting & testing

Before committing to full programme development, we build one complete course. This proves the approach works and gives your academics hands-on experience with real students.

You receive one fully designed and built course ready to launch, complete with all videos, activities, and assessments. This pilot becomes proof of concept before scaling to full programme development.

STAGE 3 | Duration: 8-12 weeks

Course planning & authoring

Our learning designers work with your subject experts to transform their knowledge into structured online courses. Through collaborative design sessions, we capture your academics' expertise and restructure it for online delivery using AI-assisted design.

You receive complete course designs for all modules: content structures, video scripts, storyboards, activity designs, and assessment specifications. Every element is aligned to learning outcomes and quality assured against academic standards.

STAGE 4 | Duration: 6-10 weeks

Course build & media production

We produce all videos, build everything in your VLE, and create all supporting materials. Our production team handles filming, editing, graphic design, and platform configuration. Your academics review and approve everything before launch.

You receive a complete online programme in your platform with all content, videos, activities, and assessments ready for student enrolment. Your team receives training on managing and updating the courses.

Typical timeline: 3-6 months from start to launch (stages may overlap)
