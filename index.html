The Problem Isn't Your Team. It's the Approach.

Most institutions approach online learning in one of two ways:

Option A

Hand it to internal teams who lack the time, tools, or specialised expertise to do it well.

Option B

Outsource to vendors who prioritise speed and cost over pedagogical soundness.

Both lead to the same place: courses that look fine but don't actually work. Learners disengage. Completion rates suffer. And your institution's reputation takes the hit.
