Here's what actually needs to happen

Online learning isn't a technical challenge. It's a teaching challenge.

Most institutions invest in recording equipment and learning platforms. But the real work is transforming how your academics think about teaching.

Classroom methods don't translate directly online. Content structure, learner engagement, and assessment all need rethinking.

That's exactly what we do. We guide your academics through this transformation whilst building everything they need to launch.

What you actually get: Ready-to-launch online courses

Not PowerPoints. Not advice. Complete courses that students can enrol in tomorrow.

Every course we build includes:

  • Complete course design: modules, lessons, activities, assessments
  • Professional video content: instructor videos, explainer animations, screen recordings
  • Interactive learning activities: discussions, case studies, simulations, group work
  • All content built inside your existing VLE (Moodle, Canvas, Blackboard, etc.)
  • Student resources: readings, templates, guides, reference materials
  • Assessment materials: quizzes, assignments, rubrics, marking guides
  • Everything tested and ready for students to start learning
You don't need to hire new staff. You don't need to buy new platforms. You don't need to train your team. We bring everything needed to design, build, and launch your programmes while your academics stay focused on their research and teaching.

From first call to students enrolled: Our four-stage process

Clear stages with specific deliverables at each step

STAGE 1 • 2-3 WEEKS

Strategy and planning

We meet with your team to understand your institution's goals, target learners, and existing resources. Together we map out exactly what needs to be built and in what order. Through structured workshops and stakeholder interviews, we identify opportunities, challenges, and resource requirements.

At the end of this stage, you receive a strategic roadmap showing all courses to be built, a detailed timeline with key milestones, resource allocation plans, and a complete implementation strategy. This roadmap becomes your blueprint for the entire project, ensuring everyone understands the scope, timeline, and what success looks like.

STAGE 2 • 4-6 WEEKS

Pilot course development

Before committing to full programme development, we build one complete course. This proves the approach works, gives your academics hands-on experience, and lets you test with real students. Your subject experts work directly with our learning designers to transform their content into an engaging online learning experience.

You receive one fully designed and built course ready to launch, complete with all videos, activities, assessments, and student materials. More importantly, you gain valuable learnings about what works for your institution, your learners, and your academic team. This pilot becomes proof of concept and gives you confidence before scaling to full programme development.

STAGE 3 • 8-12 WEEKS

Full programme design

Our learning designers work directly with your subject experts to transform their knowledge into structured online courses. We use AI-assisted design to speed up the process whilst maintaining pedagogical quality. Through collaborative design sessions, we capture your academics' expertise and restructure it for online delivery.

You receive complete course designs for all modules, including detailed content structures, video scripts, storyboards, interactive activity designs, and comprehensive assessment specifications. Every element is aligned to learning outcomes and quality assured against academic standards. These design documents become the blueprint that guides all production work in the next stage.

STAGE 4 • 6-10 WEEKS

Build, test, and launch

We produce all videos, build everything in your VLE, create all supporting materials, and test with your team. Our production team handles video filming and editing, graphic design, interactive content creation, and platform configuration. Quality assurance happens throughout, with your academics reviewing and approving everything before launch.

You receive a complete online programme built in your platform with all content, videos, interactive activities, and assessments ready for student enrolment. Nothing is left half-finished. Your courses are tested, polished, and ready to deliver an excellent learning experience from day one. Your team receives training on how to manage and update the courses, ensuring you maintain control after launch.

Typical timeline: 3-6 months from start to launch

Who we work with (and who we don't)

We're selective about the institutions we partner with

This is right for you if:

  • You're a private university, chartered institute, or well-funded organisation
  • You have clear goals and can make decisions in weeks, not years
  • You need full courses built, not just advice or templates
  • You value quality and are willing to invest properly
  • You want your academics involved throughout, not sidelined
  • You need to launch within 3-6 months, not 18-24 months

This isn't right if:

  • You only want to record lectures and upload them
  • You're looking for the cheapest option available
  • Your procurement process takes 12+ months
  • You just want advice or training, not actual course building
  • You expect perfect courses without academic involvement
  • You're not ready to rethink how online teaching works
The institutions that succeed with online learning invest properly in both the transformation and the execution. We help with both.
