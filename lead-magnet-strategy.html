The Problem

Why the current approach needs to evolve

The existing lead capture mechanism is built around a model that works for volume-driven B2C funnels. LDS operates in a relationship-driven B2B environment where the buyer is a senior HE leader — and they need a different experience.

Current Approach

"Academic Transformation Playbook" PDF

  • Gated behind an email form — friction for senior HE audience
  • Promises budget templates and cost calculators — commodity content any consultant offers
  • One-size-fits-all content — no personalisation to their institution's context
  • Generic PDF format — instantly forgettable, hard to share internally
End goal: newsletter subscriber

Proposed Approach

Three-tier funnel built around your methodology

  • Tier 1 ungated, Tier 2 optionally gated — respects the senior audience
  • Delivers a personalised readiness score mirroring Andrew's actual methodology
  • Specific and actionable — unique to LDS, not replicable by generic consultants
  • Interactive web format — shareable, indexable, designed to spread internally
End goal: qualified strategy call

Strategic Rationale

Why this must be ungated

  • Senior HE professionals despise form-filling for content that may prove generic

  • Easy to share — a champion forwards it to their committee without friction

Ends with: "Recognise any of these? Let's talk." — direct link to strategy call booking.

Tier 2 · Middle of Funnel · Key Asset

The Star Asset

Academic transformation readiness assessment

An interactive self-assessment tool (8–10 questions) that gives institutional leaders a structured framework to evaluate their online learning readiness — and gives LDS a qualified lead who has already self-diagnosed using your methodology.

Why This Is the Killer Asset

  • Your champion inside the institution needs to convince colleagues. This tool gives them a score they can present to leadership.
  • It frames the problem using your language — academic transformation, not digital transformation.
  • It creates a natural reason to talk: "We scored low on X — can you help us think through this?"
  • It mirrors your Stage 1 consulting — the assessment IS a lightweight version of what you actually do.
